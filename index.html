01 — WHO WE ARE A commitment to agricultural value
AGRICULTURE WITH INTENTION

Good things grow when the whole value chain moves forward.

Kwate Farms & Agro Allied Industries Limited is an agribusiness based in Kazaure, Jigawa State. Our work is centred on cultivation, food processing and the sale of agricultural products.

We believe agriculture can do more than produce a harvest. It can create opportunity, strengthen local value chains and help nourish communities.
